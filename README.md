# Joshua DeBellis

Mathematical physicist turned machine learning lead. I spent seven years at FINRA moving market surveillance from hand-written rules to deep learning, and building LLM agents for regulatory work.

These days I'm interested in finding the shape of things in data: what gives AI-generated content away, and how to tell when AI systems are about to go wrong.

**Featured project**
- [Spectral Fingerprints of AI Music Generators](https://github.com/joshuadebellis/ai-music-fingerprints): a 2.5 MB model that beat a 2-billion-parameter one at detecting AI-generated music on a published benchmark.
